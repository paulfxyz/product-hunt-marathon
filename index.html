§ 01 What this actually is

Not a stunt. A rhythm.

Seven small things I've been wanting to build for years, shipped in the open, on a fixed clock, with the receipts on the table.

01

Small on purpose.

Each product does one thing sharply. No dashboards, no logins, no SaaS traps. Install-and-use in under a minute.

02

Code to take further.

The seven releases have MIT source you can inspect, extend or commercialise, keeping the required notices. Open-source code is not a promise of free hosting or model usage. Commercial services are separate.

03

Costs, without the fairy tale.

These builds often cost a few hundred dollars, not a few dollars. The cards currently show illustrative AI-tool budgets and example stacks, not verified receipts or a history of which models I used. Actuals will replace the drafts.

The brief
Not a product. A call to build a founding team of 300 — for a public-good NGO offering an alternative to datacenter-first AI.
  • A non-profit association, not a startup — public good, no capture, no equity
  • Intelligence that runs on devices people already own — the datacenter as a licensed exception, not the default
  • Open standard, open cost data, DAO-voted governance — the 300 pick where it lives and what it becomes
  • You're not signing up as a user. You're signing up as a founder.
