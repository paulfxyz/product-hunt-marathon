Day 1 of my little marathon: Scribble (a made-up example), a scratchpad that opens with one shortcut and gets out of the way.

Free, open source, MIT. I built it because I kept losing quick notes in twenty open tabs.

What AI did: most of the boilerplate and the first draft of the tests. What it cost: a weekend and a few euros of API calls. What it doesn't do: sync. That's on purpose.

Feedback welcome, especially the harsh kind.
