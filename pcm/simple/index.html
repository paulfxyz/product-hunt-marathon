Di Product Hunt Marathon, for simple words

I build and ship twelve free tools inside twelve days. Dis page explain everything for simple words.

Wetin di Marathon be

Di Product Hunt Marathon run from 10 to 21 November 2026. I ship eleven tools inside eleven days, then add one bonus: Tetris. All di twelve dey free to use and dem dey open source under MIT licence, and na AI I take build dem. To build all di twelve cost about 2,200 US dollars for AI, hours and hosting, and I no buy or beg for even one upvote.

Di twelve tools

Di tools small and dem dey useful. Junk na floating scratchpad for your Mac. Mang dey suggest terminal commands and e dey wait make you confirm. Wetin Product Hunt teach me

I launch without any launch squad and I no ask anybody for upvotes, and I refuse every offer to sell me votes. To dey live and to dey seen no be di same thing: from wetin I see, product go just dey stay for about 4 points unless e get big budget for ads or editor feature am. Di editors feature Mang, but I wish say people fit see open-source work without any budget. Di full report, with di points for every launch and wetin I learn, go come out on Saturday 28 November.

SaaS don die

SaaS mean software wey you dey rent with subscription. My bet be say more companies go build and own di tools wey dem dey rent now. Na prediction, no be proof. To rebuild software no dey rebuild data, compliance or trust, and every tool need owner. Calculator dey wey go let you compare to rent and to build by yourself.
